# Made in Alipur: The Alipur Edit (new visual preview)

**Dedicated preview:** https://askwhyweb.github.io/made-in-alipur-preview/new-preview/

This is a standalone, mobile-first, editorial homepage concept. It does not replace the existing repository homepage and does not modify existing MVP data or workflows.

## Homepage
- Three-slide photographic campaign carousel with image-integrated text (no opaque coloured hero card).
- Seven image-backed navigation destinations: New Arrivals, Our Story, Collaborate with Us, Berry & Bows, Meet the Artisans, Our Collections, Journal.
- Hover previews for desktop navigation, image-thumbnail mobile menu, swipe support on mobile hero.
- New arrivals preview cards, the women and hand-embroidery story, a collaboration section, a kids sub-brand area, and a premium edit with casual/semi-formal/wedding tabs.
- Collection cards, craft journal, factory outlet editorial strip, shared footer.
- Demonstration-only wishlist, bag, article dialog, collaboration dialog, and newsletter feedback.

## Important distinctions
- **Berry & Bows** is the proposed name for the children's category.
- **Meet the Artisans** is the premium clothing line here, covering elevated casual, semi-formal, and wedding/festive looks. It is not a maker biography directory.
- All photographs, clothing names and product data are illustrative. Do not present the reference photographs as actual Made in Alipur products, outlets or artisans.
- There is no production Shopify checkout, catalogue integration, enquiry submission or newsletter service yet.
- The flower icon and typography are concept wordmark treatments pending approval against the existing brand logo assets.
